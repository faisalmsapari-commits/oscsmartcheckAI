# Spatial Planning Intelligence & RTD Trends

## 1. Overview

The Planning Intelligence layer analyzes aggregated spatial patterns across the Mukims of Langkawi:
- **Mukim Kuah:** High density, mixed commercial and institutional.
- **Mukim Kedawang & Padang Matsirat:** Coastal tourism, hotel resort developments, and airport envelope buffers.
- **Mukim Ayer Hangat & Bohor:** Low-to-medium density residential and eco-tourism.

## 2. RTD 2030 Zoning Pressure

Tracks spatial intersections with Rancangan Tempatan Daerah (RTD) Langkawi 2030 to highlight high-activity planning zones, multi-lot development patterns, and site verification statistics without disclosing individual landownership or personal cadastral data.
