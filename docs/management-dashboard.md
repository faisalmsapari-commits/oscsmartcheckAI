# OSC SmartCheck AI — Management Dashboard & Operational Intelligence

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **Management Dashboard** (`/management/dashboard`) provides senior leadership and OSC management officers of **Majlis Perbandaran Langkawi Bandaraya Pelancongan (MPLBP)** with an executive overview of:
1. Application volumes and pipeline trends
2. Planning compliance statistics and top non-compliance issues
3. Officer review queue and turnaround durations
4. Spatial planning activity and RTD 2030 zoning pressure
5. AI governance and mandatory human verification compliance (100% human sign-off)
6. Operational alert management

## 2. Dashboard Views & Tabs

- **Ringkasan Eksekutif (Executive Summary):** High-level KPI summary cards, monthly application trends, status distributions, and technical non-compliance rankings.
- **Operasi OSC (OSC Operations):** Unassigned queue, pending officer reviews, issue ageing breakdown (0-3d, 4-7d, 8-14d, 15-30d, >30d), and stage duration metrics.
- **Inteligen Perancangan (Planning Intelligence):** Mukim distribution, RTD zone activity, development pressure indicators, and common planning issues by locality.
- **Tadbir Urus AI & Sistem (AI Governance & System Integrity):** Human verification rate ($100\%$), rule provenance traceability ($100\%$), document extraction quality, and report integrity.

## 3. Strict Governance Guardrails

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Analytics is strictly descriptive and evidence-based.
> - Zero generative AI hallucinations in numerical computations.
> - Zero predictive applicant scoring or approval probabilities.
> - Zero punitive employee rankings.
> - Viewing or generating analytics never mutates existing applications, SmartCheck results, or verified comments.
